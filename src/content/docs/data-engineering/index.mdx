This section documents how data moves through systems and how those systems are made reliable.

## What belongs here

- Data ingestion and collection
- Batch and streaming pipelines
- Data validation and quality checks
- Data modeling and storage decisions
- Orchestration and scheduling
- Pipeline failures and recovery
- Freshness, latency, and cost measurements
- Backfills, retention, and operational runbooks

## Evidence to capture

For each data engineering project, document:

1. **Decisions** — source systems, storage format, schema, orchestration, and trade-offs.
2. **Failures** — bad records, schema changes, late data, duplicate events, and failed jobs.
3. **Measurements** — freshness, throughput, completeness, failure rate, and processing cost.
4. **Operations** — monitoring, retries, backfills, alerting, ownership, and rollback.

## Future project structure

Add project-specific pages under this section when you have real work to document:

```text
data-engineering/
  project-name.mdx
  schema-evolution-incident.mdx
  pipeline-recovery-runbook.mdx
```

The portfolio project should show what was built. These pages should explain the decisions, failures, measurements, and operations behind it.
